Muddle: Color Mix Sort Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Summary

Muddle does not require an account and does not collect your name, email or contacts. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device information to show and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, settings and streaks are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Muddle uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, app usage and diagnostic information

This data is used to deliver, personalize (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime via Settings → Privacy options in the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

Muddle is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
